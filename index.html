Layanan Kami

PT. Bunga Laut Mandiri adalah agen maritim full-service dengan pengalaman lebih dari 10 tahun di industri pelayaran Indonesia. Kami menyediakan layanan terintegrasi untuk berbagai jenis kapal dan kebutuhan logistik dengan tim profesional multi-bahasa.

Cruise Ship

PT. Bunga Laut Mandiri is a full-service agency with qualified and knowledgeable personnel who have long experience in the maritime industry, both afloat and ashore.

As the longest-standing port agency for more than 15 years, we have served all types of vessels, including cruise lines. Our experienced and multi-lingual team provides full vessel attendance and ensures fast, efficient handling of all details involved in a ship’s operation in Indonesian ports.

Shore Excursions

Enjoy a leisurely island tour on an executive bus, experience an exhilarating sail on sparkling azure waters, snorkel or dive the myriad of vibrant reefs, trek through mountains, and marvel at Indonesia's geological wonders.

We provide your guests with high-quality, highly professional tours that can be customized to suit your particular needs.

Stevedoring

PT. Bunga Laut Mandiri provides integrated stevedoring services to industrial clients throughout the region, equipped with loading/unloading equipment tailored to the conditions of the cargo.

Together with our subsidiary companies, stevedoring is a discipline that requires support from other elements within the scope of the overall transportation industry.

Freight Forwarding

We contract with carriers to facilitate the movement of goods.

Freight can be booked on a variety of carrier types.

Shipping Agency

Shipping Agency Service involves the fulfillment of operations regarding passengers, cargo, maintenance, supply, crew change, and more.

We are experienced in handling customs formalities, clearance, and logistics for goods traffic.
