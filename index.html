Layanan Kami

PT. Bunga Laut Mandiri adalah agen maritim full-service dengan pengalaman lebih dari 15 tahun di industri pelayaran Indonesia. Kami menyediakan layanan terintegrasi untuk berbagai jenis kapal dan kebutuhan logistik dengan tim profesional multi-bahasa.

Cruise Ship

PT. Bunga Laut Mandiri is a full-service agency with qualified and knowledgeable personnel who have long experience in the maritime industry, both afloat and ashore.

As the longest-standing port agency for more than 15 years, we have served all types of vessels, including cruise lines. Our experienced and multi-lingual team provides full vessel attendance and ensures fast, efficient handling of all details involved in a ship’s operation in Indonesian ports.

Stevedoring

PT. Bunga Laut Mandiri provides integrated stevedoring services to industrial clients throughout the region, equipped with loading/unloading equipment tailored to the conditions of the cargo.

Together with our subsidiary companies, stevedoring is a discipline that requires support from other elements within the scope of the overall transportation industry. To enhance client satisfaction with our commitment to excellence in all processes, we have adopted the Quality Management Standard ISO 9001:2008, certified by IAPMO-USA on February 02, 2011.

Our capabilities in stevedoring, warehousing, vessel ownership of various types and ranges, ship operating, and shipping agency all contribute to the success of our company group. With our presence in major ports across Indonesia, we assure seamless stevedoring services for the benefit of our clients.

Freight Forwarding

We contract with carriers to facilitate the movement of goods. As experts in supply chain management, we act as a "travel agent" for the cargo industry or a third-party logistics provider to move manufactured goods.

Freight can be booked on a variety of carrier types, including ships, airplanes, trucks, and railroads. It is not unusual for a shipment to move along its route using multiple carrier types.

Freight Forwarding services include:

  • Project Logistics Planning, Design, Budgeting, and Consultancy
  • Customs Clearance
  • Port Handling
  • Land and Sea Transportation
  • Loading and Unloading

Shipping Agency

Shipping Agency Service involves the fulfillment of operations regarding passengers, cargo, maintenance/repair, survey, supply, crew change, loading/discharging, pilot/tugboat services, and more for naval and commercial vessels and vehicles calling at Indonesian ports.

We are experienced in handling customs formalities, clearance, excise duties, and all logistics related to the traffic of goods. We have excellent experience in yacht shipments, project facilitation, project cargoes, and local logistics.
